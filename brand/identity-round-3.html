Where I land

Wordmark 2, with Wordmark 1 as its long-form sibling. Wordmark 2 is the polycrisis move executed with a mark that means something: the O is half world, half screen, and it lifts straight out as the favicon and the app icon. Wordmark 1 is the same system stretched across the word, and it is the one to use on the research pages, where before and after is the whole subject.

The typeface is Archivo at its widest and heaviest, which is what gives it the weight of the reference without borrowing its Montserrat-ish softness. The last plate shows the same O in Spectral, if you'd rather the institute lean scholarly than activist.

Cobalt only ever lands on the O. Never on the letters.
