Where I land

F (Phi) and M (Before / After) are the two I would put next to A from round one. Phi is the most ownable thing on either page: a letter the field already uses, with the screen driven through it. Before / After is A with the bezel removed, which makes it sit better next to a serif wordmark.

E (Minutes Away) is the best story and the worst logo. It wants to be the hero illustration on the landing page, animated, not the mark. I (Interference) is the same: keep it for the site, not the door.

If you want the center to feel like it has a building, pair any mark with Setting 2 of the wordmark.
