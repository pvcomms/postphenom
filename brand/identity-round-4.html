Hand 4, the correction, is the idea. Hand 1 is the mark that goes with it. The correction says what the center is in one gesture: a discipline that already existed, amended by hand after the fact. Nobody else can have it, and it gets better the messier the handwriting is.

Which is the real point. A handwriting font is still a font, and a trained eye clocks it. The version that is actually not AI is your own hand: write "post" and "postphenom" ten times each on plain paper with a brush pen or a thick marker, photograph the sheet in daylight, and I'll trace the best one into a vector and drop it into Hand 4 and Hand 1. Then the logo is yours in the literal sense, and no one can say a machine drew it.

Ink is blue-black, the fountain-pen colour, on the same cool paper. No accent this round; ink on paper is the whole palette.
