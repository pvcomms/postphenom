Reading while listening

Three short passages: one to read, one to hear, one to read while it is read to you.

Four questions after each, with no looking back. Then your three side by side, and what the studies found for people like the ones in them. It keeps nothing.

  1. Athree roundsRead, heard, and both at once, in an order drawn for this sitting. The passages are invented, so nothing you already knew answers for you.
  2. Bside by sideHow many of the twelve you answered as the passage had it, each way; your pace and the voice's; how far chance alone would spread three rounds.
  3. Cthe channelsSeven ways to split words and pictures between the eye and the ear, drawn, with what the studies found for each.
