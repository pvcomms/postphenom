Alarm

Will this set off your fight or flight?

Name what trips your alarm, what fires when it does, and what brings it down. Mark a pathway against them, then poke a toy body and watch which road it takes.

The claim

You flinch before you decide. The low road reaches the alarm before the slow one reaches the part of you that appraises, and how loaded you are today sets how much room the brake has. So whether a decision will set you off is not a forecast anyone can make for you. You already half know it: the triggers it touches, the defences that fire, the brakes within reach. Named in your own words and added up, those marks draw a line toward hypervigilance or toward calm, and nothing more than that.

    the reading

    what the alarm holds to

    How it is read: name what sets your alarm off, what fires when it does, and what brings it down; then mark a pathway against them and poke it. The body here is a toy; the names are yours. A touched trigger bends the pathway’s line up by how hard it hits times how much of it is on the path; a brake within reach bends it down by how fast it works, with less room the more loaded you are today. The line is your marks added up and nothing else. The run is the sandbox’s physics, unchanged: the low road wins the race, sleep debt and stress lower the brake’s ceiling, hold the alarm too high and it flips to freeze.

    It never scores a person, ranks a pathway or says which road you will take. Nothing is guessed in: a trigger you did not name does not bend the line, and the reading counts and dates and never grades. No host is called, no model is asked, and nothing you write leaves this browser.

    Honest limits
    • The body is a toy: a handful of equations for an amygdala, a prefrontal brake, a vagal brake and a sympathetic surge. Its gauges are illustrative, and its heart rate is nobody’s.
    • The line is your marks added up. If you leave a trigger unnamed, the line does not know about it, and it will not guess.
    • A brake that works over hours or days barely reaches a run this short. The page says so when you press one, rather than pretending.
    • It never predicts you. After the fact you can say how a pathway went, and the reading counts what you said beside what you marked. It does not grade the difference.

    What stays here

    Your circuit and your pathways are kept in this browser’s storage and nowhere else. There is no server, no account and no analytics. The specimen is fiction, written to show every mechanic; it is nobody’s nervous system.
