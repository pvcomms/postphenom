Mask

Write the other side's case as they would put it, to be read by them. Then mark each sentence for what you could mean. Where the mask slips is where you stand.

An ideological Turing test turned inward: write the other side's case as they would put it, then find out which of it you could mean.

It counts the tells in each voice, says which of your values each case leans on and what the two share. Counts, never a grade. It never says whether the mask passes, or which side is right.
