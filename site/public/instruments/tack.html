Tack

You cannot sail straight at what is so. Hold a heading, see what the water does, correct — a tack at a time. Beliefs in the sails, loose. Commitments in the hull, fixed.

It opens on a specimen. Specimen A is a made-up person with one belief in the sails and one commitment in the hull. Open them and read them; make your own to set down something of yours.

It never says whether a claim is so, how likely, or what to hold.
