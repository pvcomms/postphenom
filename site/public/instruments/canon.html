Canon

How often a note is reached for is a count, and a count is not a meaning. Whether what gathers round it is one story, whether the idea moved along it, whether it is of use and whether it belongs in the canon are judgements, and only the reader can make them.

Pick a root, a note three or more others reach for. The strand is drawn along time: what reaches for it above the line, its nearest kin by words below. Ring the touchpoints, up to ten; the page offers a few, dashed, each with why. Strike what is not the story. Say whether it is one story, whether the idea moved along it, whether it is of use, each in a line if you want. Write the story. Then the call: into the canon, or leave it out.
