Wish

May you be safe, well, at ease, happy. Said to yourself, then outward, ring by ring, to everyone.

The claim

Loving-kindness is something said, not something you have to feel. The wishes are said, and what they do is theirs.

Your people on rings that widen from you to everyone, the one you find difficult only as far as you can hold them today. Truths that hold for every being, said of each one, so saying one of someone is not a claim about them. Facts you keep about yourself. The page counts sittings and who was held; it never says whether you are kind.
