Slice

Meet sixteen people a little at a time. See what the first second did to your calls, and what more of the same did to how sure you were.

The claim

A first second is enough to form a view of someone, and it stays on the page at every call after. More of the same can make you surer without making you right more often. Whether a first impression is worth trusting depends on the room it was formed in, and how sure it feels is no reading of that.

    What it leaves to you

    The page counts what the first second did to your calls, and what more of the same did to how sure you were. Which room a first impression was formed in, and whether it is one where the first second is any guide, it cannot say, and neither can how sure it felt. That is yours to weigh, each time.
