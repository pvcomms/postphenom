what the distribution holds to

it never says which tail is the good one, never puts one thing you weighed above another, and never calls a thing worth reading. its one guess at your call is sealed until you have made it, and is scored against your own base rate, not against you. nothing is fetched, no model is asked, and nothing leaves this page.
