02

The senses

Hover a card. The channel it travels down lights on the body. None of the channels is neutral.

The red of a stop sign, then a brake light, then a badge. Red means alarm because of a century of red meaning alarm.
The pager on the belt, then the phone in the pocket, then the pulse under your thumb. A buzz is a summons because it has always been one.

Texture has a history. Change the history and the same cue lands at a different intensity.

Poke
Switch the preset, then hover the buzz card and the hail. The channel is the same. The weight is not.

Downstream

07

The loop closes

The world acts on you through the world's perception of you. Here is the perception.

Every dwell, tap and skip in this session moved these four weights. The next card served was chosen from them. The feed you scrolled at station one was already this.

Poke
Zero them. The feed goes flat. That flatness is the baseline everything above was measured against.
