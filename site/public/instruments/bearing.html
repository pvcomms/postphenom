Bearing

Your values, drawn as one sheet. Set a decision down where you judge it sits and read what that placement says. The sheet surfaces; you decide.

The claim

Where a decision sits among your values is a judgment, and a sheet cannot make it for you. What a sheet can do is take the judgment you made with your hand and read it back in words you can argue with: which values it serves, which it is silent on, what the overlap is called, and how the judgment moved since you first set it down.

Nothing on the sheet ranks or scores. Outside every circle is allowed to be the honest answer. A heading drawn from a decision says what the move would gain, leave and keep, and stops there. The notes about each value are found by matching the words you gave it against titles, tags and first lines, never bodies: a value named once in passing is not what a note is about.

Your sheet, its values and its decisions, is kept in this browser and nowhere else. A folder of notes you open is read in the page, never uploaded, and forgotten when you leave.
