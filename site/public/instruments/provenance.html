How did this reach you? The hands a claim came through, what each one said, and what each one gains.

The claim

Whether a claim is so is not something an instrument can know, and one that pretended to would be a horoscope with a straight face. What you can know is how it reached you: every hand it passed through, what each one said, on what channel and on what day, what each gains if you believe it, and what it runs on.

Put the claim down in the words it arrived in, and as it was first said if you can find that. Name the hands in between. The sheet draws the chain from the first saying to you. A hand is drawn solid only where you went to it and checked it yourself, and hollow where you took it on trust. Then the wordings are read back as words: what each one gained and lost on the way, and which words the claim leans on. Nothing here decides.

It opens on a specimen: an invented claim about remote work that reached an invented person through four hands. Press n to put down one of your own. What you write is kept in this browser and goes nowhere else.

        what the provenance holds to

        Solid is a mark you make, not a thing the page can see. It means you went to the hand and read what it said for yourself. Everything else reached you on trust, which is how most of what anyone knows reaches them. The chain is the hands you can name between the first saying and you, drawn solid where you checked a hand yourself or saw with your own eyes, and hollow where you did not; the wordings are compared as words, the census counts the usual ones, and the interests are what you wrote about each hand. A check settles what it settles and nothing more.

        Nothing here says whether the claim is so, likely, credible or trustworthy. No hand is ranked above another, no question or check is written for you, no link is fetched and no model is asked. What you write is kept in this browser and goes nowhere else.

        • The census is a fixed list of English words in seven families. A wording can lean hard without any of them, and a careful one can use them honestly. It counts; it does not judge.
        • The drift compares words, not meanings. A faithful paraphrase still shows words gained and lost, and a turn that keeps the words but changes the sense shows nothing.
        • The chain is the hands you can name. The ones you cannot remember are not on it, and they are usually the feed.
